## About Me

Hi there! My name is Matt and I am a Research Fellow in the [Dynamics Research Group][1], University of Sheffield. I am broadly interested in the fusion of data and physics, to build **interpretable**, **explainable** and **physics-informed** models to understand and monitor critical engineering infrastructure. I largely work in the areas of aerospace and transport, with projects ranging from monitoring landing gears to aero-engines. 

## Research Interests

- **Digital twins** for predictive maintenance
- **Trustworthy and explainable modelling** using Gaussian processes and physics-informed machine learning
