## About Me

Hi there! My name is Matt and I am a Research Fellow in the [Dynamics Research Group][1], University of Sheffield. I am broadly interested in the fusion of data and physics, to build **interpretable**, **explainable** and **physics-informed** machine learning **models** for understanding and predicting how engineering infrastructure behaves. I largely deploy these models in areas such as predictive modelling for aerospace, bridge and rail monitoring applications. 

## Research Interests

- **Explainable modelling:** physics-informed learning, Gaussian processes
- **Digital twins:** structural health monitoring, system identification
